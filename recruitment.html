Roles & Areas of Interest

Applicants can choose their primary areas of interest on the registration form. Discover what each role entails:

Electronics & IoT

Soft Robotics

Electronics, IoT & Hobby Projects

Microcontroller programming (ESP32, Arduino), sensor interfacing (IR, ultrasonic, IMU), motor drivers, wireless bot telemetry, line followers, maze solvers, and creative hobby projects.

ESP32 & Arduino IoT Systems Sensor Arrays Hobby Projects
Heavy Mechanics

Combat Robotics

Mechanical Components, Machining & Assembly

Design and fabrication of high-impact bots for Robowars, Robosoccer, Sumo, and Race. Machining high-durability chassis, custom weapon mechanisms, drivetrains, and robust metal assembly.

Robowars Robosoccer Sumo Race Machining
CAD & Prototyping

3D Designing

CAD Modeling & Rapid Prototyping

Parametric 3D modeling using Autodesk Fusion 360 & SolidWorks. Conceptualizing custom chassis geometry, kinematic stress simulation, slicing, and 3D printing components.

Fusion 360 SolidWorks 3D Printing Slicing
Design & Media

Graphics Designing / Editing

Visual Identity, Posters & Video Production

Creating high-impact event posters, identity mockups, social creatives, promotional reels, after-movie edits, and branding graphics for official workshops and competitions.

Photoshop Illustrator Premiere Pro Video Editing
PR & Branding

Social Media

Community Engagement & Digital Outreach

Managing official club channels (@roboticsclub_nitte), authoring engaging event announcements, driving follower growth, live tournament coverage, and student outreach.

Instagram LinkedIn WhatsApp Channel Content Strategy PR Outreach
Aero & Marine

Rocketry / Drones / Boats

Aero Dynamics, UAVs & Marine Robotics

Designing quadcopters and fixed-wing drones, solid-propulsion model rocketry, autonomous RC boat hulls, and aerodynamic payload testing.

Drone Assembly Model Rocketry RC Marine Bots Aerodynamics

⚡ Selection Rounds

Stage 01

Round 1 – Aptitude Test

Evaluates logical reasoning, basic electronics/mechanical thinking, quantitative aptitude, and core problem-solving capacity.

Stage 02

Round 2 – Interview

Shortlisted candidates will interact with the core team to discuss domain passion, project ideas, adaptability, and club commitment.
